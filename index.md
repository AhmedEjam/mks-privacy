Privacy Policy for My Knowledge Space (MKS)

1. Data Collection and Storage
My Knowledge Space (MKS) is an offline-first application. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal user data, analytics, or usage statistics over the internet.

2. Local Data
All data generated by the app, including imported library files (ZIP, JSON, XLSX), quiz statistics, session history, and images, are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage.

3. Device Permissions
The app may request access to your device's camera or storage. This access is used solely for the internal functioning of the app, such as importing external quiz files or taking photos for local reference. No media or files ever leave your device.

4. Changes to This Policy
If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted at this URL.
