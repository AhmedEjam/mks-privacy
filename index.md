Privacy Policy for My Knowledge Space (MKS)

1. Data Collection and Storage
My Knowledge Space (MKS) is designed as an offline-first application. We do not actively collect, transmit, or share any personal user data, analytics, or usage statistics over the internet.

2. Local Data & AI Integrations
All user-generated data, including imported library files (ZIP, JSON, XLSX), quiz statistics, session history, and images, are stored strictly locally on your device. If you configure the app to connect to an external Artificial Intelligence provider (e.g., via an API key), only the specific prompts you generate will be sent to that provider in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

3. Device Permissions
The app may request access to your device's camera or storage. This access is used solely for internal functioning, such as importing external quiz files or scanning text locally. No media or files are transmitted off your device by us.

4. Third-Party Services
The app utilizes standard third-party infrastructure (such as Google Firebase for remote configuration or push notifications). These services may automatically collect basic, anonymized device identifiers to function properly.

5. Changes to This Policy
If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted at this URL.
